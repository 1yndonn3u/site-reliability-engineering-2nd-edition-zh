Praise for Site Reliability Engineering, 2nd ed.

各界对 Site Reliability Engineering（第 2 版）的赞誉

How do you edit a timeless classic in the field of SRE, or maybe more importantly, why? And how do you do it during a moment of profound change in our world where it feels like anything we write might as well be written using disappearing ink? The short answer is Google, along with the rest of the SRE world, continued to learn after the first edition was published. The SRE Book, 2nd ed., brings us those lessons, including where it works to focus for success with SRE, what we got wrong, and how the past can inform us as we navigate the unevenly distributed future we find ourselves in.

要修订 SRE 领域一部不朽的经典，该从何下手？或许更关键的是，为什么要修订？而且是在这样一个世界剧变的时刻——我们写下的任何文字，仿佛墨迹未干便已褪去——又该如何动笔？简短的回答是：第 1 版出版之后，Google 连同整个 SRE 世界都在持续学习。本书（第 2 版）把这些经验带给了我们——包括 SRE 应当把精力聚焦在何处才能成功、我们做错了什么，以及当我们在自己所处的这个“分布并不均匀的未来”中前行时，过去能给我们怎样的启示。

David N. Blank-Edelman, author of Becoming SRE; editor/curator of Seeking SRE; and cofounder of SREcon

David N. Blank-Edelman，Becoming SRE 作者；Seeking SRE 编者/策划人；SREcon 联合创始人

It’s been ten years since the first edition of the SRE Book and I have to admit that my copy has drifted to the bookshelf on the far side of my office: useful, certainly, but not one I need to reach from my desk every day. So when I got this new edition, I expected to skim past familiar topics to find some new techniques (presumably about AI). Instead, I found myself reading for hours: there are smart ideas about AI reliability for sure, but new ideas everywhere else as well. The state of the art has come a long way in ten years and it’s all in here.

SRE 第 1 版面世至今已有十年，我得承认，我那一本早已渐渐挪到了办公室另一头的书架上：有用，当然有用，但已不是我每天都要从书桌边伸手去取的那一本。所以当我拿到这个新版时，本以为只会快速翻过那些熟悉的主题，去找几样新技法（多半是关于 AI 的）。不料，我却一读就是好几个小时：这里固然有关于 AI 可靠性的精妙见解，但其余各处同样处处是新意。十年间，业界前沿已大步向前，而这一切尽在书中。

This is a deep, thorough, practical guide from authors who have spent their careers getting really good at building and running reliable systems. One for the bookshelf right beside the desk.

这是一本深入、详尽而又实用的指南，出自一群用整个职业生涯把构建与运维可靠系统磨炼到炉火纯青的作者之手。值得摆在书桌旁触手可及的那层书架上。

Tanya Reilly, principal engineer; author of The Staff Engineer’s Path

I’m particularly excited by the way this updated text addresses AI in SRE. This topic usually brings so much hype and exaggeration on one hand and negativity and denial on the other. This is a must-get second edition of a must-have work.

最让我兴奋的，是这一版探讨 SRE 中 AI 问题的方式。这个话题往往一边是铺天盖地的炒作与夸大，另一边则是消极与否认。本书用翔实的案例，讲解了 AI 在事故管理、可观测性、容量规划，当然还有软件工程中的运用。但最重要的是，它正面回应了两个相互关联的问题：SRE 需要做些什么来驾驭 AI 系统，以及 AI 又能如何重塑我们在 SRE 中的工作。这是一部必备之作的第 2 版，同样不容错过。
