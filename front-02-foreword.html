Foreword

序言

The first edition of Site Reliability Engineering was published a decade ago, and at the time we weren’t sure how much interest there would be in its content. The SRE Book, as it became known, captured much of our accumulated knowledge, experiences, best practices, and ideas for operating Google-scale distributed systems. However, we were concerned that what we wrote about might be too esoteric or Google-specific to be broadly useful. We thought that perhaps a few thousand people might read our book, and fewer still would put it into practice.

Site Reliability Engineering 第 1 版出版于十年前，当时我们并不确定其内容会引起多大的兴趣。这本后来被大家称为“SRE Book”的书，汇集了我们在运维 Google 规模分布式系统方面所积累的大量知识、经验、最佳实践与思路。然而，我们当时也担心，书中所写的内容也许过于深奥，或过于 Google 特有，难以具有广泛的实用价值。我们原以为，大概只会有几千人读到此书，而真正将其付诸实践的人还要更少。

I am pleased to say that we may have slightly underestimated the interest in our material. Adoption exceeded our wildest expectations, and it has become one of O’Reilly’s most popular books. Engineers and organizations across the industry have incorporated ideas, terminology, and challenges from the SRE Book into their own thinking and practices, often finding their own way of implementing, incorporating, or extending the concepts we covered. In the same period, SRE as a discipline has grown and matured, largely outside of Google itself. Within Google SRE, we are both proud of what we have achieved and happy that we have been successful in sharing it.

可以欣慰地说，我们当初大概稍稍低估了大家对这些内容的兴趣。它被采纳的程度远远超出了我们最大胆的预期，本书也成了 O’Reilly 最受欢迎的图书之一。业界的工程师与组织把 SRE Book 中的理念、术语与挑战吸收进自己的思考与实践，往往以各自的方式去落地、吸纳或拓展我们所讲述的概念。同一时期，SRE 作为一门学科不断成长、走向成熟，而这在很大程度上发生在 Google 之外。在 Google SRE 内部，我们既为自己的成就感到自豪，也为能成功地把它分享出去而感到欣慰。

With another decade of SRE behind us, we also have several new observations about the profession and industry that are worth sharing and that form the subtext for much of what is in this second edition.

又经历了十年的 SRE 实践之后，我们对这个职业与这个行业也有了几点新的观察，值得在此分享；它们也构成了本书第 2 版诸多内容背后的潜台词。

First, we find it notable that the growth of SRE has not come at the expense of other approaches to systems management. The You-Build-It-You-Run-It (YBIYRI) approach continues to be a default for many organizations, including several inside Google, and is often the optimal choice early in a service’s life. Many of the technical principles covered in the SRE Book—progressive rollout, circuit breakers, cascading failure protection—are independent of the organizational principles, and services that incorporate these technical principles often find less early utility in also adopting SRE organizational structures. This is a sign of progress!

首先，值得一提的是，SRE 的发展壮大并没有以牺牲其他系统管理方式为代价。“谁构建、谁运维（You-Build-It-You-Run-It, YBIYRI）”模式仍是许多组织（包括 Google 内部的一些团队）的默认做法，而且在服务生命周期的早期往往是最优选择。SRE Book 中讲到的许多技术原则——渐进式发布、熔断、级联故障防护——本身并不依赖于 SRE 的组织形式；服务只要用上了这些技术原则，早期再额外引入 SRE 的组织架构，收益往往就没那么大了。这正是进步的体现！

With this continued heterogeneity of operational models, however, we have also observed cases where services and teams that started as YBIYRI have grown to the point where SRE’s organizational design would be more efficient for them, but inertia or even bias impedes the change. Here, a quantitative benchmark is useful to help teams assess their current state. As of this writing, across Google:

然而，在这种运维模型持续多样化的同时，我们也观察到这样一些情形：一些最初采用 YBIYRI 的服务与团队，已经发展到改用 SRE 的组织设计会更高效的程度，却因惯性甚至偏见而迟迟未能转变。此时，一个量化的基准有助于团队评估自身的现状。截至本书撰写之时，在整个 Google 范围内：

  • SRE is less than 4% of total engineering headcount.

    SRE 人数不到全体工程人员的 4%。

  • Production work consumes less than 15% of total engineering time.

    生产工作占用的时间不到全部工程时间的 15%。

These figures represent a moment in time, and we expect them to further improve over the coming years as we move more toil work into automation. However, we have found this quantitative benchmark to be compelling, since many software as a service (SaaS) teams spend 30%+ of their total engineering time on production work and toil. Faced with the evidence that a sub-4% team can reduce engineering toil by half, the provocative question “Would you like to free up more than 10% of your headcount for feature development?” is often persuasive. Few engineering leaders will turn down extra engineers, and the roughly 4:1 return in engineering cycles on an investment in SRE is a straightforward way for them to get those extra engineers. SRE’s core proposition has thus become even stronger as evidence, technology, and practice have accumulated.

这些数字只是某一时刻的快照；随着我们把更多琐事工作交给自动化来完成，预计未来几年它们还会进一步改善。不过，我们发现这一量化基准颇具说服力，因为许多软件即服务（SaaS）团队要把总工程时间的 30% 以上花在生产环境工作与琐事上。面对“一个占比不到 4% 的团队就能把工程琐事减半”这样的证据，“你是否愿意腾出 10% 以上的人力去做功能开发？”这个尖锐的问题，往往很能打动人。很少有工程负责人会拒绝额外的工程师，而在 SRE 上的投入能换回约 4:1 的工程人力回报，这正是他们获得这些额外人手的一条直接途径。于是，随着证据、技术与实践的不断积累，SRE 的核心主张变得愈发有力。

Second, over the last decade we increasingly encountered the limits of reliability by policy, and we have been replacing it with reliability by default. We gradually observed that SRE teams across Google had successfully developed automation and tooling for specific classes of tasks, but with three key challenges:

其次，在过去十年里，我们越来越多地触及“靠策略保障可靠性”这一做法的局限，并一直在用“默认即可靠”来取而代之。我们逐渐发现，Google 各处的 SRE 团队都成功地为特定类别的任务开发了自动化与工具，但其中存在三大关键难题：

  1. Individual SRE teams developed tools that solved their specific use cases but did not work for all other teams. Thus, instead of a single way to (say) roll out a configuration change globally, there were several distinct solutions, each tailored to a subset of systems: stateless versus stateful, regional versus global, etc. Each system had a large enough group of users that it was self-sustaining, and each tried to gain additional adherents in order to get more developers contributing to its codebase.

    各个 SRE 团队各自开发的工具，只解决了自己特定的使用场景，却无法适用于其他所有团队。于是，针对（比方说）在全局范围内发布一次配置变更这件事，并不存在唯一的做法，而是有好几种各不相同的方案，每一种都是为某一类系统量身定制的：无状态与有状态、区域级与全局级，等等。每套系统都拥有数量足够多的用户，足以自我维系；而每套系统又都在设法争取更多拥护者，以吸引更多开发者为其代码库做贡献。

  2. As our collective understanding of reliability principles continued to evolve, each individual system often did not immediately implement the latest findings. Thus, SRE had an excellent set of policies governing reliability—for example, “all rollouts to 99.95%+ reliable systems shall be progressive”—but it was up to many individual teams to translate these policies into their specific tools and correctly implement them, and any team that used a tool or system that hadn’t implemented the latest policies would be exposed to increased reliability risk.

    随着我们对可靠性原则的集体理解不断演进，各个单独的系统却往往没有及时落实最新的认识。于是，SRE 拥有一套出色的、用以治理可靠性的策略——例如“对可靠性达到 99.95% 以上的系统，所有发布都必须是渐进式的”——但要把这些策略落实到各自特定的工具中并正确实现，仍要仰仗许多独立的团队；而任何团队，只要使用了尚未实现最新策略的工具或系统，就会面临更高的可靠性风险。

  3. Finally, since reliability tooling was up to individual SRE teams, there was no need for them to adopt the existing systems. If a new type of service didn’t neatly work with one of the existing tools, SRE teams were free to create their own—often missing several of the hard-won lessons encoded in existing systems, and again increasing their services’ reliability risk.

    最后，由于可靠性工具的建设全凭各个 SRE 团队自行决定，他们也就没有必要非得采用现有系统。一旦某种新型服务无法与现有的某个工具顺畅配合，SRE 团队便可以自由地另起炉灶、打造自己的工具——而这往往会遗漏那些已沉淀在现有系统中、来之不易的经验教训，再次抬高其服务的可靠性风险。

The combination of these three issues led to both duplicative engineering work—N systems each implementing K policies—and a number of avoidable outages. The latter issue was eventually dubbed “Treynor’s Lament” after I repeatedly observed during postmortems that “There are no new outages at Google, only new instances of old outages.” The solution was simple, though not easy:

这三个问题叠加在一起，既造成了重复的工程劳动——N 套系统各自重复实现同样的 K 项策略——也导致了一些本可避免的故障。后者最终被称为“Treynor 之叹”，得名于我在一次次事后总结中反复说的一句话：“Google 没有新故障，只有老故障的重演。”解决之道说来简单，做起来却不容易：

  1. Standardize all teams on a single system for each class of reliability practice (e.g., configuration change management).

    针对每一类可靠性实践（例如配置变更管理），让所有团队统一到同一套系统上。

  2. Mandate that all SRE teams use only that system for automating their changes.

    强制要求所有 SRE 团队只能使用那一套系统来自动化其变更。

The combination of these two requirements was called Convergence and was a program that spanned much of the past decade. Convergence was combined with an increased extra-SRE development focus on common tooling; together, the two produced what we now call the Google Production Platform (GPP). We call this combination reliability by default, because any new SRE team will adopt GPP, extend it as necessary to work with its specific service, and in so doing inherit all of Google’s collective reliability experience.

这两项要求合在一起，被称为 Convergence（收敛），它是一项贯穿了过去十年大部分时间的计划。Convergence 还与 SRE 之外、对通用工具日益加强的研发投入相结合；二者共同造就了我们如今所称的 Google 生产平台（Google Production Platform, GPP）。我们把这种组合称为“默认即可靠”，因为任何一个新的 SRE 团队都会采用 GPP，并按需加以扩展以适配其特定服务，在此过程中便继承 Google 集体积累的全部可靠性经验。

It is worth noting that the Convergence change was not popular with the SRE teams for which it required changing systems, and thus more short-term work! However, there is no arguing with the result: with an SRE team that has maintained a fixed <5% ratio of total engineering headcount, Google has observed an integer (>2!) step-function improvement in its total outage frequency × scope × duration since implementing Convergence.

值得一提的是，Convergence 这项变革，在那些因此需要更换系统、从而平添了不少短期工作量的 SRE 团队中并不受欢迎！然而，结果却不容争辩：在 SRE 团队始终保持低于 5% 的全体工程人员占比的情况下，自实施 Convergence 以来，Google 观察到其故障总量——以频率 × 影响范围 × 持续时间衡量——出现了整数倍（而且不止 2 倍！）的阶跃式改善。

Third, we continue to find that the early decisions to hold a consistent hiring and skills bar for SRE and product developers but to keep the SRE organization distinct from engineering are both critical to the team’s ability to deliver the earlier-cited 4:1 engineering efficiency gain of an investment in SRE relative to YBIYRI. Part of the benefit comes from SRE teams’ ability to maintain focus and priority: while feature needs often change quarter to quarter (and sometimes more frequently), SRE teams can remain focused on and personally invested in optimizing reliability and feature velocity. Another part of the benefit is that SREs regularly move out of the team and into other parts of Google software engineering, bringing with them both the experience and the culture from SRE.

第三，我们持续发现，早期做出的那些决定——对 SRE 与产品开发者坚持一致的招聘与技能门槛，却让 SRE 组织独立于研发体系之外——对于团队能否兑现前文所述的、投资 SRE 相对于 YBIYRI 所带来的 4:1 工程效率提升，都至关重要。这种收益，一部分来自 SRE 团队保持专注与明确优先级的能力：功能需求往往每个季度都在变化（有时甚至更频繁），而 SRE 团队却能够始终专注于、并亲身投入到可靠性与功能迭代速度的优化之中。另一部分收益则在于，SRE 工程师会定期从团队中流动出去，进入 Google 软件工程的其他部门，并把源自 SRE 的经验与文化一并带了过去。

Finally, the first edition of Site Reliability Engineering opened with “It is a truth universally acknowledged that systems do not run themselves.” Part of what is so exciting about our current historical moment is that machine learning is becoming increasingly capable of doing, or automating, ever more of the manual production toil that SREs still tend to. The opportunities for ML to multiply the effectiveness of each SRE are legion, including AI agents looking through the codebase to find all the places where remote procedure calls do not include exponential backoff and fixing them; taking postmortem action items and looking for similar patterns or latent booby traps throughout the entire codebase, not just the system being reviewed; performing automated STPA risk analysis for all systems, not just the handful we have cycles for now; and so much more. It is a heady time for SRE, and I look forward to our progress over the next decade with both excitement and optimism.

最后，Site Reliability Engineering 第 1 版开篇就写道：“系统不会自己运行，这是一条举世公认的真理。”当下之所以如此令人振奋，原因之一在于：机器学习（ML）越来越有能力替 SRE 工程师代劳或自动化那些至今仍靠手工处理的生产琐事，而且能接手的范围还在不断扩大。ML 能让每位 SRE 工程师的效能成倍提升，这样的机会比比皆是。比如：让 AI 智能体扫遍代码库，找出所有没加指数退避的远程过程调用（RPC），并逐一修复；拿着事后总结里的行动项，在整个代码库里排查有没有同类问题或还埋着的雷，而不只盯着本次复盘的那个系统；对所有系统做自动化的 STPA 风险分析，而不只是眼下人手顾得过来的那几个；还有很多很多。对 SRE 而言，这是一个激动人心的时代；对未来十年的进展，我既兴奋又乐观。

Benjamin Treynor Sloss
